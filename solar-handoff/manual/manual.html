A · What the system does
Section A

What the system does

The Solar Bill Review System turns a homeowner's online form into a completed Solar Bill Review report, automatically. It reads every utility bill, checks it, fills in the report, files everything in the customer's folder and tells your team when it is done.

Step 1

Homeowner submits

Bills, a pre-solar bill and the production file, through your online form

Step 2

Files checked

PDF only, the same utility account, a clear billing month and year

Step 3

Bills read

Grid import, grid export and the billing month taken from each bill

Step 4

Report updated

The report is filled in and every accepted file is saved in the customer's folder

Step 5

Team notified

An email with the report link, the folder link and the PDF attached

A bill that fails any check is left out of the report and listed in the notification, so your team always knows what was used and what was not.

B · Submitting a request
Section B

What the homeowner submits

Homeowners send everything through your online form. A complete request has four parts.

1

Contact details and contract

First name, last name, email and phone number, plus the contracted solar production (kWh).

The phone number identifies the customer. Every later submission must use the same number, so new bills are added to the same report.

2

Monthly utility bills

Up to 12 bills, one per month, as PDF files downloaded from the utility's website.

Any order works. The system reads the billing month from each bill, so a bill uploaded in the "wrong" month box still lands in the right month.

3

A bill from before solar

One bill from before the solar system was installed, showing the 12-month usage chart.

The system reads all twelve bars of the chart to fill the pre-solar comparison.

It is checked first: it must be from before solar, for the same utility account, and its chart must not run past the start of the first solar bill. The last bill before solar is ideal.

4

Solar production file

The production export from the homeowner's monitoring portal, as an Excel file (.xlsx) with one row per month.

It fills the solar production for each month. Once received, it is kept for later submissions.

One customer, one number. If a homeowner submits with a different phone number, the system treats them as a new customer and starts a new folder and report.

How a bill finds its month

The upload box is only a hint. The month printed on the bill decides where it goes.

Getting submissions right first time

Most review emails come from the wrong kind of file. These points prevent them.

Utility PDF
downloaded from the utility's website
Photo of a bill
cannot be read reliably
Screenshot
image files are ignored
Another account
rejected to protect the report

Not accepted

  • Photos or scans of paper bills
  • Screenshots and image files (JPG, PNG)
  • Bills from a different utility account than the rest
  • Documents that are not electric bills, such as contracts or gas bills
  • Partial bills without the meter readings page

For the best result

  • Download each bill as a PDF straight from the utility's website
  • Upload the complete bill, not just the first page
  • One bill per month box; the order does not matter
  • Export production by month, not by day
  • Always use the same phone number

Suggested text for your form page

Place this above the upload fields so homeowners know exactly what to send.

Copy-ready

Please upload your electricity bills as PDF files downloaded from your utility's website. Photos and screenshots cannot be read.

Upload one bill for each of the last 12 months. The order does not matter. Please also upload one bill from before your solar system was installed, and your solar production report exported from your monitoring app as a monthly Excel file.

If you send more bills later, please use the same phone number so they are added to your existing report.

C · What happens next
Section C

What happens next

The system starts the moment the form is submitted. Nobody needs to press anything.

1
Submitted
The form arrives and joins the queue
2
Checked and read
Each file is checked, then read one by one
3
Report updated
The report is filled in and files are saved
4
Email sent
Usually 2–4 minutes for 12 bills, about a minute for a few

One request at a time

Requests are processed in the order they arrive, one at a time. This keeps each customer's report consistent and prevents a double submission from creating two reports. When several full requests arrive together, each waits for the one before it: five full requests take about 15 to 20 minutes to clear. When the bill-reading service is busy, a single full request can take up to 8 minutes; nothing needs to be done, the email still arrives.

Resubmissions update the same report

Same number, same report

A submission with the same phone number goes into the customer's existing folder and report.

Only what is new is added

Months already on file stay. The homeowner only needs to send what is missing.

Newer bills win

A newer bill for a month replaces an older year's bill, and a corrected (re-issued) bill replaces the original. An older bill never replaces a newer one.

Complete at twelve

The report is complete once all 12 months are on file and nothing is flagged. Your team then receives "Solar report ready".

G · Do's and don'ts
Section G

Do's and don'ts

The system runs on its own. These few habits keep it that way.

Do

Keep one phone number per customer. It is how every submission finds the right report.
Ask for PDFs from the utility's website. It prevents most review emails.
Resubmit only what is missing. Months already on file stay.
Review every "needs review" email before anything goes to the homeowner.
Check the Run Log whenever you are unsure what happened to a request.

Don't

Rename, remove or add upload fields on the form. The system recognises each field by its exact name.
Edit, move or rename the report template or the Customer Reports folder.
Delete or rename a customer's folder or report. Later submissions would have nowhere to go.
Type over figures the system fills in. The next submission may overwrite them.
Change the automation or its connected accounts. Contact Ubakel for any change.

If you remember only three things

01
One number per customer
The phone number links every submission to the right report.
02
PDFs from the utility
Never photos or screenshots.
03
Nothing renamed
Form fields, template and folders stay exactly as they are.
