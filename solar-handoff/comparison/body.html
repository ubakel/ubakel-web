Our recommendation

Best value

Keep Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite

About $10 a month. Already built and tested on real bills. Nothing to change.

If you want more accuracy

Gemini 3.8 Flash

Google's newest Flash model. A simple switch plus a re-test, no rebuild. $18, then $35 a month.

If you prefer Claude

Claude Sonnet 5.5

About $50 a month. Strong on tables, but the bill-reading steps must be rebuilt and re-tested.

At this volume every sensible option costs between $10 and $60 a month, about the same as the Make plan. A single wrong number in a customer report costs more than the difference. Before switching, we suggest running the same real test bills through the two or three finalists and comparing every figure. That test costs only a few dollars.
Why not DeepSeek. Its API doesn't accept PDF files, so every bill would need an extra conversion step. Its privacy policy says personal data is processed and stored in China, and these bills carry homeowners' names, addresses and account numbers.
Privacy. Paid Gemini, Claude and OpenAI API accounts don't use your data to train their models. Gemini's free tier does, so a paid key is required.
How the costs were estimated. About 15,000 tokens read and 3,000 written per bill, 13 bills per full request. Providers count PDF pages differently, so treat the figures as a comparison, not a quote.
