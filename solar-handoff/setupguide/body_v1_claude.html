Steps 1 and 2
Step 1 · about 5 minutes

Upgrade your Make plan

Make is the service that runs the system. Your Make account is on the Free plan, which stops every run after 5 minutes and covers only about 5 full requests a month. A full request takes 2–4 minutes, and up to 8 when things are busy.

  1. Sign in at make.com.
  2. Open Organization → Subscription, or click Upgrade.
  3. Choose the Core plan with 20,000 credits per month.
  4. Pay by card. Paying yearly instead of monthly saves about 15%.

The extra room covers resubmissions and follow-up bills. Please keep Ubakel's access to your Make account until the system is live.

Step 2 · about 2 minutes

Choose one Google account

The system saves reports in Google Drive and sends your team's emails from Gmail. Pick one Google account for all of it. A shared company account, such as reports@yourcompany.com, is best, so the system doesn't depend on one person.

Works

Any @gmail.com address, or a company address run by Google Workspace. Both have a Gmail inbox.

Tell us first

If your company email runs on Microsoft 365, Zoho or another provider. We'll send the team emails another way.

If your company uses Google Workspace, your IT admin may need to allow Make. If Google shows "blocked by your admin" in Step 5, that's why.

Set up your Drive folder

Everything the system creates lives in one folder in your Google Drive. Ubakel will share two files with the Google account from Step 2: the Report Template and the Run Log. When it's done, your folder looks like this:

NetSolar – Solar Reportsyou create this
Report Templateyour copy of the file we share

One folder per customeradded automatically
  1. In Google Drive, click New → New folder and name it NetSolar – Solar Reports.
  2. Open the Report Template we shared with you (you'll find it under Shared with me). Click File → Make a copy, name it Report Template, choose your NetSolar folder, then click Make a copy.
  3. Do the same for the Run Log, naming it Run Log.
  4. Inside your NetSolar folder, click New → New folder and name it Customer Reports.
  5. Send Ubakel the links to the three items: right-click each one, choose Share → Copy link, and paste the links into an email to us.
You can rename or move the folder and files later. The system will still find them.
Don't delete them, and don't change the layout or tab names inside the template. The system fills in exact cells.

Step 4
Step 4 · about 10 minutes

Open your Claude account

Claude, made by Anthropic, reads each bill and pulls out the numbers for the report. You pay Anthropic directly for what you use. There's no monthly fee.

  1. Go to platform.claude.com and sign up with your company email.
  2. When asked, create your organization using your company name.
  3. Open Settings → Billing. Add a payment card and add credit. $50 covers about one month at 50–70 full requests. Turn on automatic reload if it's offered, so the credit never runs out.
  4. On the same page, set a monthly spend limit of $100. Claude stops at that amount, so the bill can't surprise you. If it's ever reached, bills can't be read until you raise it, and your team gets a "needs review" email listing them.
Do the last two steps right before Step 5, with our link already open, so the key goes straight into Make and never has to be stored anywhere.
  1. Open Settings → API keys and click Create key. Name it NetSolar – Make. Choose no expiry if it's offered, or the longest option. Then click Create.
  2. Copy the key (it starts with sk-ant-) and paste it into the Claude box on our link (Step 5). It's shown only once; if it's lost, just create a new one.
Treat the key like a password. Never send it by email, chat or WhatsApp, not even to us.
Your customers' privacy: under Anthropic's commercial terms, bills sent through the Claude API are not used to train Claude.

Step 5
Step 5 · about 10 minutes

Connect your accounts

We'll send you one link. It opens a page in Make listing four accounts to connect. You sign in yourself, so we never see your passwords.

  1. Open the link. If Make asks you to sign in, use your usual Make login.
  2. Click each of the four items in turn. For the three Google items, choose the same Google account each time (the one from Step 2). If Google shows tick boxes, tick all of them, then click Allow.
  3. For Claude, create your key (Step 4, steps 5–6), paste it in and save.
  4. When all four show as connected, reply to our email to let us know.

Longer bills cost more: one full request ranges from $0.60 to $1.40. Even at the high end, 70 requests stay under the $100 limit.

Why Claude Sonnet 5.5

Bills are full of small numbers in tables, and a wrong number in a customer report costs far more than a few cents. Sonnet 5.5 reads them accurately at a moderate price.

Claude Opus 5.5 is the most careful option, at about twice the Claude cost ($85–120 a month). We don't recommend the cheapest model for numbers that go into customer reports.

These are estimates. We'll confirm the real cost per request during testing. Prices as of October 2026: Claude Sonnet 5.5 costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, where a token is about ¾ of a word (Anthropic's published API prices); Make prices are from make.com/pricing. Prices can change.
