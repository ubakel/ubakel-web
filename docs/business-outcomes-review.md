# Business outcomes homepage review

English and Arabic homepages now lead with practical results: less repeated work, quicker report preparation, fewer manual handoffs and clearer responsibility. Examples remain starting points for a custom workflow, rather than a fixed service menu.

## Confirmed commercial positioning

- The business owns the automation system built for it.
- Testing lasts two weeks or longer depending on the project.
- Monthly retainers are recommended by default but optional. Customers can opt out at any time and keep ownership.
- Account access, handover and support coverage belong in the agreed project scope.
- No fixed prices, delivery promises, support response times or quantified savings have been invented.

## Customer experience checks

- English and Arabic layouts checked at 320, 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixel widths; no horizontal page overflow found.
- Required fields and malformed email validation checked in both languages. Errors are inline and focus moves to the first invalid field.
- Department tabs, Arabic keyboard direction, workflow comparison, FAQ disclosure and contact close controls checked.
- Example rotation is now visitor-controlled. Replay and animation pause controls remain available.
- Client workflow illustrations, demonstration content and intended benefits are distinguished from measured project results.
- No valid enquiry was submitted. Email delivery and production service availability were not tested.

## Files

`src/lib/homepage-content.ts` holds the English and Arabic outcome, ownership, support and buying guidance. `src/lib/homepage.ts` assembles these into the existing branded templates and contact form. Interaction and responsive presentation live in `public/homepage.js` and `public/homepage.css`.
